## Functional explanation

Status: **documented**

Role: service&#95;certificate&#95;store&#95;template

SystemCertificates locates the certificate system stores associated with a named service. The enclosing service placeholder must be resolved to understand ownership. A system store is a logical collection that may include multiple physical stores, so the registry location should not be treated as a complete list of effective trust material. Identify the concrete service, store and certificate before interpreting an observed certificate-management operation.
