## Functional explanation

Status: **structural**

Role: custom&#95;storage&#95;policy&#95;namespace

Custom groups the documented read-denial and write-denial policy branches for administrator-specified removable-storage classes in the current-user policy tree. Deny&#95;Read and Deny&#95;Write here are child keys with their own policy state, rather than interchangeable names for one parent switch. A preserved Microsoft ADMX template also places a class list beneath each operation branch. This parent organizes those related policies and does not itself establish which storage classes are selected.

## Atomic claim
