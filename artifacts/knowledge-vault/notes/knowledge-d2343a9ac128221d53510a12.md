## Functional explanation

Status: **documented**

Role: winrm&#95;client&#95;authentication&#95;policy

This machine WinRM Client policy key stores the administrative setting governing direct use of Kerberos by the Windows Remote Management client. Microsoft maps DisallowKerberos&#95;2 to AllowKerberos here. Enabling the disallow policy prevents direct Kerberos selection, but Kerberos may still be selected through Negotiate. The client location concerns authentication sent to remote services, rather than credentials accepted by the local WinRM service.

## Atomic claim

Controls direct Kerberos authentication by the machine WinRM client.

Scope: **machine**

## Meaning in system-level logs

Inspect AllowKerberos data together with operation and result, interpreting the inverse wording of DisallowKerberos. To identify actual authentication, require a WinRM connection record including target, negotiated mechanism, and outcome.

### What the path cannot establish

- This setting alone cannot establish that every possible use of Kerberos by WinRM is disabled.
- The key does not show an outbound session, credential transmission, or successful authentication.
