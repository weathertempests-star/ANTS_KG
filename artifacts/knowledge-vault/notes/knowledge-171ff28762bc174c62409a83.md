A service-name child selects a service-specific certificate namespace. This placeholder is the service identity slot in the Cryptography&#92;Services path. Certificate storage is represented by the SystemCertificates child, while ordinary startup configuration belongs to a separate SYSTEM tree. A log lookup must bind a real service name before assigning ownership; the template itself neither installs a service nor identifies a certificate.
